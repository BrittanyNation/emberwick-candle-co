Good to know

The questions people actually ask at the counter.

Each candle lists its burn time on the shelf — most run 40 to 55 hours. Burn for 2 to 3 hours at a time so the wax melts evenly, and trim the wick to a quarter inch before each lighting. Every batch is burn-tested, and any candle that tunnels or smokes gets replaced — just tell us.

Our own delivery covers Clarksville and the surrounding area. For anywhere else in the country, we ship by mail — candles travel well in our padded boxes, and shipping is free over $60 just like delivery.
